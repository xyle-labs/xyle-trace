Public workflow brief, based on the recorded example

Goal: compare published planned assembly occurrences in Berlin, Dresden and
Rostock for 30 October through 5 November 2026 inclusive, Europe/Berlin.
Calculate listed scheduled occurrences per 100,000 official residents. Create
an actual map and a source-to-KPI graph of the recorded provenance.

The source references in sources.json identify the frozen research versions
used in the video. Their original response bodies and event records are not
included. Live source URLs can change; do not claim they reproduce the original
versions without matching captured hashes. For a new study, choose an explicit
current window and supply source copies whose use and storage are authorized.

Work in a separate consuming project. Apply the installed Trace skills and the
provided events/population evidence contracts. Capture the actual source bytes
before relying on them, preserving origin and retrieval representation. Bind
observations to precise row, index, line or cell locations. Do not seed evidence
from the published graph IDs or infer backing from structural proximity alone.

Use one common dated official population vintage for all three cities; keep its
date visible and distinguish official totals from municipal register figures.
Count dated scheduled starts, not attendance or organisers. Retain repeated
vigils on different listed dates. Exclude exact duplicates and explicitly
cancelled/prohibited entries, recording the rules and excluded totals. Missing
or unavailable coverage is unknown, not zero.

Publication rules and representations matter. Dresden excludes strikes and can
omit short-notice assemblies. The recorded Rostock input was a lower-fidelity
research-tool text extract, not original HTTP bytes; preserve that distinction.
A retained snapshot establishes which bytes were used, not source completeness
or factual accuracy. Do not silently substitute a different version.

Register only the scoped observations needed for the analysis. Use coherent,
precisely located evidence batches. Validate the actual inputs before
calculation, and retain code/concrete inputs with xyle-trace-run when the
standard-library Python/JSON contract fits. Attempt a matching retained replay
and independently verify the arithmetic and source counts.

For the map, use verified city coordinates and contextual geography. City rates
are not an interpolated surface over unsurveyed areas; streets are not a
population denominator. Keep display-source origins distinct from numeric
inputs. The source graph must project real recorded paths, with readable labels.

Finish with results, source/version limitations and actual validation/health
outcomes. Distinguish managed numeric replay from reported presentation runs.
Preserve real failures and unresolved gaps; do not weaken the policy or modify
inputs merely to obtain a desired total. Any claim review is by the agent unless
a human actually reviews it. Publish only a separately reviewed, minimized view.
